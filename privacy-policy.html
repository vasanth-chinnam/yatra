Welcome to Yatra — a free India travel and tourism guide. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information. By using Yatra, you agree to the practices described here.

📋 1. What Information We Collect

Information you provide:

  • Account details (name, email) when you register
  • Wishlist items, saved routes, and preferences stored locally

Automatically collected:

  • Browser type and device type
  • Pages visited and time on site
  • General location via IP address (country/region)
  • Referring URLs

Note: All user data (wishlist, routes, login) is stored only in your browser's localStorage. Nothing is sent to our servers.

🎯 2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the Yatra experience
  • To understand how users navigate the site (analytics)
  • To serve relevant ads via Google AdSense
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

🍪 3. Cookies & Local Storage

  • localStorage: Stores your wishlist, saved routes, and session on your device only — never transmitted.
  • Cookies: Google Analytics and AdSense may set cookies to measure traffic and personalise ads.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

🔗 4. Third-Party Services

  • Picsum Photos / Unsplash — Destination images
  • Leaflet / OpenStreetMap — Maps (ODbL)
  • Google Fonts — Typography
  • Google Analytics — Anonymous traffic data
  • Google AdSense — Display advertising (see below)

📢 5. Google AdSense & Advertising

Yatra uses Google AdSense to display ads. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your browsing history.

  • Opt out of personalised ads: Google Ads Settings
  • Network-wide opt out: NAI Consumer Opt-Out
  • Google's data policy: How Google uses data

🔒 6. Data Security

Since Yatra stores all personal data locally in your browser, your data is protected by your own device. We don't operate a backend database storing your personal information. Third-party services we use follow strong security practices.

👶 7. Children's Privacy

Yatra is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe this has occurred, contact us and we will delete it immediately.

✅ 8. Your Rights

  • Access: Know what data is stored about you
  • Delete: Clear your data via browser Settings → Clear browsing data
  • Opt out: Disable personalised ads via Google Ads Settings
  • Contact: Ask us anything about our data practices

📝 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when we update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of Yatra constitutes acceptance.
