5 Epic India Road Trips You Must Do in Your Lifetime

📅 June 2025 ⏱ 10 min read ✍️ Yatra Travel Team

There is no better way to discover the absolute scale, diversity, and chaotic beauty of India than from behind the wheel. A road trip in India is a sensory adventure — a journey where the languages, cuisines, landscapes and road signs shift every few hundred kilometres. From the highest, oxygen-thin mountain highways of the Himalayas to smooth coastal drives along the Arabian Sea, India has some of the most epic driving routes in the world.

Whether you're an avid motorcyclist, a family road-tripper, or looking to hire a car and driver, hitting the road offers a front-row seat to the real India. Here are 5 bucket-list road trips you absolutely must do at least once in your lifetime.

🗓 Road Safety in India: Driving in India requires intense concentration and quick reflexes. Avoid driving at night due to poor lighting and high-beam glare. Keep an eye out for speed bumps, potholes, and stray animals. When in doubt, hire a professional local driver.

The 5 Epic Drives

1

The Leh-Manali Highway — Roof of the World

This is the holy grail of Indian road trips. Connecting the lush valleys of Himachal Pradesh with the high-altitude cold desert of Ladakh, this 474-kilometre highway is an extreme test of endurance and mechanical capability. You will cross 5 high-altitude mountain passes — including Rohtang, Baralacha La, and Tanglang La (5,328m) — and drive across the vast, flat Gata Loops (a series of 21 hairpin bends). The landscape shifts from alpine pine forests to barren, moon-like rock formations, with spectacular views of glaciers and snow-capped peaks.

📏 Distance: 474 km ⏱ Duration: 2 days (with overnight stay in Keylong/Sarchu) 📅 Best Season: June to September
2

Mumbai to Goa Coastal Drive — The Konkan Highway

Trade the mountains for the sea on the ultimate beach-bound road trip. Connecting the financial capital Mumbai with the sun-kissed sands of Goa, this drive along the NH-66 Konkan highway is a tropical dream. The route takes you through the heart of the Konkan region, passing mango orchards, coconut groves, old Maratha forts, and sleepy fishing villages. Take the scenic state highways rather than the national highway to stay closer to the coastline and cross estuaries on river ferries.

📏 Distance: 600 km ⏱ Duration: 10–12 hours 📅 Best Season: October to March
3

Bangalore to Coorg via Mysore — Coffee Country Cruise

A perfect weekend getaway that takes you from India's tech hub into the rolling hills of the Western Ghats. Starting from Bangalore, you'll drive along the smooth Expressway to Mysore (famous for its grand palace), and then climb into the mist-covered hills of Coorg (Kodagu). The final stretch of the drive is incredibly sensory, winding through dense teak forests, cardamom plantations, and acres of emerald-green coffee estates under a canopy of shade trees.

📏 Distance: 270 km ⏱ Duration: 5.5 hours 📅 Best Season: July to February (glorious in the rains)
4

Shimla to Spiti Valley via Kinnaur — The Cliffhanger

If you think the Leh-Manali highway is wild, Spiti will blow your mind. Often described as the "most treacherous road in the world," this route starts from the colonial hill station of Shimla, tracks the rushing Sutlej River, and winds along roads carved directly into sheer granite cliffs in Kinnaur. You'll drive through the iconic "half tunnels" before entering the Spiti Valley, a cold desert home to ancient Tibetan monasteries (Key, Tabo), high-altitude villages (Komic, Langza), and stunning vistas of the Spiti River.

📏 Distance: 450 km ⏱ Duration: 3–4 days (highly gradual to avoid AMS) 📅 Best Season: June to October
5

Chennai to Pondicherry — The East Coast Road (ECR)

For a short, incredibly smooth, and highly scenic coastal cruise, the East Coast Road (ECR) is unbeatable. Hugging the Bay of Bengal coastline all the way, this two-lane highway connects the capital city of Chennai with the French-quarter charm of Pondicherry. Along the way, you can stop at the UNESCO World Heritage monuments of Mahabalipuram (famous for its rock-cut shore temples), enjoy panoramic views of salt pans, and grab fresh seafood at seaside shacks.

📏 Distance: 170 km ⏱ Duration: 3.5 hours 📅 Best Season: October to March

Planning Your Road Trip

Vehicle Choice: High-clearance vehicles (SUVs, Crossovers) are highly recommended for mountain routes like Leh-Manali and Spiti, where river crossings and gravel roads are common. For coastal drives and expressways, standard sedans or hatchbacks are perfectly fine.

Navigation: Download offline Google Maps before heading into the mountains, as mobile networks (especially non-postpaid connections) can be completely unavailable in remote areas of Ladakh, Spiti and Northeast India.
